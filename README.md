## Description
This project aims to analyse guest satisfaction and facility service performance at PEACEACE, a hospitality service provider, using Power BI. The project delivers an interactive dashboard that provides visibility into guest feedback, service quality, and loyalty metrics across different demographics, visit purposes, service categories, and time periods.
Using Power Query for data preparation and Power BI for visual analytics, the dashboard helps identify service gaps, monitor performance trends, and support data-driven decision-making.
Through this analysis, the project aims to enhance service quality, improve the guest experience, increase loyalty, and strengthen PEACEACE’s reputation for excellent hospitality services.

### Business Introduction
PEACEACE operates in the hospitality industry, offering accommodation and facility services to guests visiting for business, functions, and leisure. The organisation serves a diverse guest base with varying expectations across rooms, restaurants, staff interactions, and general facilities.
In a highly competitive hospitality environment, guest satisfaction plays a critical role in repeat visits, positive reviews, and long-term brand loyalty. To sustain service excellence, PEACEACE requires a structured, data-driven approach to evaluate performance and continuously improve guest experience.

### Business Problem
Despite consistent guest traffic, PEACEACE is currently facing:
- Variations in guest satisfaction across facility service areas.
- Limited visibility into guest feedback trends and satisfaction drivers.
- Low Net Promoter Score (NPS), indicating weak guest advocacy.
- Difficulty identifying which services most impact guest experience.
- Reactive service improvements driven by complaints rather than insights.
- These challenges make it difficult for management to prioritise improvements and maintain consistently high service standards.

### Rationale for the Project
Guest satisfaction directly impacts customer retention, brand reputation, and revenue in the hospitality industry. Poor service experiences can lead to negative reviews, reduced loyalty, and loss of competitive advantage.
This project focuses on guest satisfaction analysis because:
- High-quality service is essential for repeat business.
- Data-driven insights help prioritise service improvement efforts.
- Understanding guest behaviour supports better operational decisions.
- Proactive service improvements strengthen PEACEACE’s market position.

### Aim of the Project
This project focuses on developing an interactive Power BI dashboard to evaluate guest satisfaction and facility service performance. Through this, I aim to:
- Monitor overall guest satisfaction and facility ratings.
- Identify underperforming service areas.
- Analyse guest feedback across demographics and visit purposes.
- Measure guest loyalty using Net Promoter Score (NPS).
- Support data-driven service improvement initiatives.
- Improve guest experience and long-term retention.

### Tech Stack
Tool: Power BI (Power Query & DAX)
- Dashboard Development:
 Creating interactive guest satisfaction and facility service performance dashboards.

- Data Processing:
  Using Power Query to clean, transform, and prepare guest feedback data, including standardising ratings, handling missing values, and creating calculated columns.
  
- Data Visualisation:
 Building charts, tables, KPI cards, and gauges to analyse guest satisfaction, facility ratings, and Net Promoter Score (NPS).

- Reporting:
 Implementing filters, slicers, and dynamic views to explore guest satisfaction metrics by demographics, visit purpose, service category, and time period.


### Project Scope and Workflow
Data Preprocessing
- Cleaned and transformed raw guest feedback data using Power Query.
- Standardised rating scales and categorical fields.
- Handled missing, inconsistent, and duplicate records.
- Prepared data for efficient modelling and analysis.

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Analysed satisfaction trends by year and quarter.
- Explored guest demographics, including age group and gender.
- Examined guest distribution by visit purpose and booking source.
- Identified patterns in facility service ratings.

Data Transformation
- Created calculated columns for age ranges and NPS classification.
- Aggregated ratings into overall satisfaction and facility scores.
- Derived time-based metrics for trend analysis.
- Prepared KPIs for dashboard reporting.

Data Analysis
- Assessed service performance across rooms, restaurants, staff, and facilities.
- Analysed NPS distribution (promoters, passives, detractors).
- Identified service areas contributing to dissatisfaction.
- Evaluated guest behaviour across different segments.

Data Visualisation
I created visual elements such as:
- Overall guest satisfaction and facility rating KPIs.
- Net Promoter Score (NPS) gauge and segmentation visuals.
- Guest breakdown by age group and gender.
- Guest distribution by visit purpose and booking source.
- Facility service performance tables.
- Yearly and quarterly performance trends.

### Dashboard Design
I designed a fully interactive Power BI dashboard featuring:
- Slicers for year, purpose, and service category.
- Dynamic KPIs for real-time performance monitoring.
- Clear visual hierarchy for executive decision-making.
- User-friendly layout for easy interpretation of insights.

### Insight Generation
Through the dashboard and analysis, I derived insights into:
- Overall guest satisfaction levels and performance gaps.
- Facility service areas requiring immediate improvement.
- Guest loyalty patterns based on NPS results.
- Demographic and purpose-based satisfaction differences.
- High-impact guest acquisition channels.
- Opportunities to enhance service quality and guest experience.

### Recommendations
I documented the entire process and provided actionable recommendations to:
- Improve low-rated service areas such as food variety, room cleanliness, and internet services.
- Implement targeted staff training to improve guest interactions.
- Convert passive guests into promoters through service excellence.
- Strengthen guest feedback follow-up processes.
- Use NPS insights to guide loyalty and retention strategies.
- Continuously monitor service performance using Power BI dashboards.
